How to play

Make two teams, each with a clue-giver and a guesser. Only the two clue-givers may see the password: shield the phone and hold to reveal it, one person at a time.

The active team gives one spoken word as a clue; their partner gets one guess. A wrong guess or an illegal clue passes play to the other team and lowers the value: 6 → 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1. After six missed turns, draw a new password.

Clues cannot contain the password or a form of it, be multiple words, spell it out, or use gestures. Saying the password ends the word and gives the other team 2 points. Other illegal clues cost the turn.

Before each new word, the trailing team may privately check the password and choose Play (give the first clue) or Pass (let the opponents go first). Passing leaves the word worth 6 points. When scores are tied, the team that last guessed correctly starts again. The opening team is chosen above; if no team has guessed correctly yet, a tie keeps the previous starting team.

Turns are untimed. Swap clue-giver and guesser within each team between words. The first team to reach 15 or more wins. Players judge clues and guesses aloud; use the buttons to record the result.
